# Forecast source follow-ups

- Research official global and regional fire-danger forecast services as
  complements or alternatives to the FRMv3 demonstration WMS.
- Compare geographic coverage, forecast horizon, update frequency, licensing,
  machine-readable access, operating status and outage notifications.
- Distinguish weather-based danger forecasts from satellite fire detections and
  official incident reports. Do not equate different risk scales without evidence.
- Research only initially; no automatic fallback or new provider is approved yet.
